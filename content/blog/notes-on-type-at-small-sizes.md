Headlines get the care, but the small text carries the product: labels, timestamps, captions, the line under a button.

## Three rules

1. **Never go below 12px for anything people need to read.** Legal copy included.
2. **Loosen tracking as size drops.** Tight letter spacing that looks sharp at 48px turns muddy at 12px.
3. **Use colour, not weight, for hierarchy.** A muted grey separates metadata from content without adding visual noise.

Small type is where a design either feels considered or doesn't.
